Players build from [kits](/docs/mechanics/items-crafting/crafting-stations/#kits-place-stations). Small camp and
gathering structures may stand anywhere. Houses, crafting stations and town buildings only go up in a settlement, next
to what already stands there. So the open world does not fill up with forges and walls, and towns grow in one piece.

{{< alert context="info" text="**Conventions used on this page.** Distances are in metres on the ground, measured from where a structure is placed." />}}

# Wild and Town Structures

| Class    | Structures                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Where           |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| **Wild** | Campfire, chest, workbench, mana circle, mining shaft, theodolite                                                                                                                                                                                    | anywhere        |
| **Town** | Wall, floor and stairs pieces, furnace, forge, tanning rack, loom, glass furnace, glassblowing bench, alchemy laboratory, gem cut station, transmutation workbench, carving table, mint, breeding farm, incubator, blackboard, post office, auction house, storage center | in a settlement |

Every new kind of structure is a town structure unless it is added to the wild list.

The [city sign](/docs/mechanics/towns-trade/player-settlements/#founding) has rules of its own: it founds the
settlement.

# Building in a Settlement

A town structure needs a settlement, and inside it, it must stand within **30 m** of something that is already there.

| Settlement                                                                 | The settlement is               | Within 30 m of                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Player settlement](/docs/mechanics/towns-trade/player-settlements/)       | the sphere around the city sign | the city sign, or a finished building of the town                                      |
| Generated town or hamlet                                                   | the town's ground               | the town centre, a generated building that still stands, or a finished player building |
| [Guild Stronghold](/docs/mechanics/society/party-guild/#guild-strongholds) | not designed yet                | not designed yet                                                                       |

A **building** here is a finished town structure that earns
[building points](/docs/mechanics/towns-trade/player-settlements/#the-sphere-of-influence): a crafting station, a
wall, floor or stairs piece, a post office, an auction house, a storage center or an inn. A wild structure is not, not even a workbench: a lone
workbench in the open starts no town. Neither is a construction site.

So a town grows outward, one building next to the last. A player building in a generated town anchors the next one too,
so players can add a street to a hamlet. They cannot build past the town's own ground.

A generated building that was destroyed anchors nothing.

Whoever walks into a settlement sees its name, a player town's too. That is also how the game knows where a town ends.

{{< alert context="info" text="A post office, an auction house, a storage center and an inn also need a **player** settlement, and a town takes one of each. See [Player Settlements](/docs/mechanics/towns-trade/player-settlements/)." />}}

# Before the Click

While a kit is in hand, its ghost turns **red** where the kit may not go, and a line above it says why: no settlement,
too far from the town, or too close to another master's structure. A click there does nothing.

# Building in the Wild

Wild structures have no limit, but they need room: outside every settlement, a structure must stand at least **10 m**
from every structure of another master. Your own structures may stand close together, so a camp with a campfire, a
chest and a workbench is fine. Inside a settlement there is no gap.

# Clearing Away Structures

Anybody may knock down a structure they can attack. A [ward stone](/docs/mechanics/society/pvp/#protected-zones)
protects one as it protects everything else a player owns. Whoever destroys **another master's finished** structure
gets:

- **25 %** of the materials of its kit, rounded down, dropped as loot for them.
- [Work experience](/docs/mechanics/bestias/bestia/#work-experience) for **25 %** of the kit's build time.

The one who dealt the most damage counts, with a master and their Bestia together. A construction site gives nothing,
and neither does destroying your own structure. A chest still spills what it holds on top.

The experience follows the build time, so a campfire is worth next to nothing and a forge little more. Building
something to knock it down again never pays. The reward is for clearing away what others left behind. An owner who
wants to keep a far-away mining shaft leaves a Bestia there to watch it.
