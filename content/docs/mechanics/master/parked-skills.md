These sub-trees were cut from the [skill tree pages](/docs/mechanics/master/basics/#skill-trees) to reduce the scope of the initial
release. This page is never rendered. Each section below is the original text, unchanged, so it can be pasted back as is.

To unpark a sub-tree:

1. Paste its section back into its tree page at the place given in the table. Sub-tree headings are `#` there, not `###`.
2. Un-comment its node and its `Gate -.->|unlocks|` arrow in the parent tree diagram (marked `%% parked`).
3. Restore its mention in the parent tree's intro text.

Nothing is parked at the moment.

Anchors inside these sections (`#skill-...`, `#craftsman-tree`, ...) point into the tree pages and only resolve once the
section is back there.
