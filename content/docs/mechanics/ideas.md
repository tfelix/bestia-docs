This page is a scratchpad for ideas that came up along the way but haven't been fully thought through yet. Some of them may grow into real features, others may turn out to be completely impractical — that's the nature of a brainstorm. Have an idea of your own? Feel free to add it below!

# Idea List

- **Twitch Integration** - Hook Twitch chat and events into the game engine (possibly via the _Twitcher_ Godot addon), so streamers and their viewers can influence the world. One idea worth exploring: crediting the viewer who inspired a crafted item by attaching their name to it.
